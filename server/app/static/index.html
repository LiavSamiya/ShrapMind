Describe the fragment case

ShrapMind works out what a retained fragment is made of, searches 505 curated enzymes for a candidate that could erode that material, and simulates it inside a product-capturing hydrogel — then refuses to recommend anything that fails a safety gate.

Research simulation. Every result is a hypothesis requiring wet-lab validation. This system models material chemistry; it does not provide clinical guidance, dosing or treatment direction, and it will refuse requests for them.

Expect refusals. No turnover number in the knowledge base was ever measured on a solid fragment — of 289 kinetic measurements, 168 are on chromogenic model esters and none on the real material — so the provenance gate blocks most rate claims by design. That is the finding, not a fault.
Simulation only — not clinical guidance.
